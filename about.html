About Food Delivery System

Our Food Delivery System is a user-friendly platform designed to simplify online food ordering. Customers can browse menus, place orders, and manage their cart easily through a responsive interface. The frontend is developed using HTML and CSS, while the backend functionality such as order management, dynamic updates, and interactivity is implemented using JavaScript.
